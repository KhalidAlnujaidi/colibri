## What was delivered

- **Phases 1–3** (env-gated; every measured config 200/200 token-exact on
  `ref200.json`): TierRecent ring + rolling Bloom victim guards; TierMarkov
  transition-chain prefetch staging; `DEMAND_POLICY=lru|lfru|auto`,
  `GROUP_EVICT` affinity eviction, `EXPERT_DIRECT`, `PILOT_WORKERS`,
  `ALIGN_SLOTS`.
- **Backport** to GLM/Qwen3.6 (`de1682fd`), including the qwen36 `Slot.hold`
  guard.
- **Cold-disk verdict**: with the page cache defeated, the PILOT verdict flips —
  `pilot2_markov2` beats demand (+12.5% tok/s, P99 −55% in the interleaved
  Phase B); PILOT=3 and `PILOT_WORKERS=4` regress; Markov is the decisive win
  once misses cost real device latency. macOS caveat: `F_NOCACHE` cannot force
  device reads of resident pages (UBC still serves them).
- **Kitchen-sink cold result**: `lfru+GROUP_EVICT+EXPERT_DIRECT+PILOT=2+MARKOV_TOP=2`
  = **4.817 tok/s median at cap=8** (+10.0% vs `pilot2_markov2`, +21.6% vs
  demand; 200/200 exact, one SHA). LFRU neutralizes prefetch's extra hits —
  staged heat-0 slots are evicted before use (~176 prefetch hits, currently 0);
  `PILOT_EVICT_GUARD` only guards pilot-vs-resident.

Full details, tables and exact commands:
`docs/experiments/olmoe-tier-caching-2026-09-29.md`.
